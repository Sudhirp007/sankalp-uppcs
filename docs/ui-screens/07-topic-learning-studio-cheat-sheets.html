High-Recall Article Blueprint (Art 243 to 243-O)

UPPSC matches Articles directly with clause definitions in Paper 1 Question Papers.

Article 243-A Village Level

Gram Sabha

A body consisting of persons registered in the electoral rolls of a village. Exercises powers & performs functions as provided by the State Legislature.

Article 243-D High Trap Rate

Reservation of Seats

Mandatory reservation for SCs & STs in proportion to population. Not less than 1/3rd of total seats reserved for women (including SC/ST women).

Article 243-I Every 5th Year

State Finance Commission

Constituted by the Governor every fifth year to review financial position and recommend distribution of net proceeds of taxes, duties, and tolls.

Article 243-K Constitutional Body

State Election Commission

Superintendence, direction and control of preparation of electoral rolls and conduct of all panchayat elections. State Election Commissioner is appointed by Governor.
