Constitutional Article & Concept Recall Front Prompt

Under which Constitutional Article is the State Finance Commission constituted every 5 years to review the financial position of Panchayats, and who appoints it?

verified Asked in UPPCS 2018, 2021, 2024 • High-Yield Revision Node
Answer Revealed (Press Space to Re-Flip)
check_circle Canonical Answer

Article 243-I (for Panchayats) and Article 243-Y (for Municipalities) — Appointed by the Governor of the State.

warning Common Examiner Trap

Aspirants frequently confuse Art 243-I (Finance Commission) with Art 243-K (State Election Commission). Also critical: The State Legislature (not the Constitution directly) determines by law the composition and qualifications of members.

lightbulb Chronology & Memory Hook
I = Income (Art 243-I governs State Panchayati Finance/Income)
K = Kingmaker / Elections (Art 243-K governs State Election Commission)
