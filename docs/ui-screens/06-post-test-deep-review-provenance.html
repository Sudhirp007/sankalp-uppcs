Authoritative Canonical Explanation & Statutory Reference

Primary Source Verified
check_circle
Statement 1 is CORRECT: The Ashok Mehta Committee (appointed in Dec 1977 by the Janata Government, submitted report in August 1978) explicitly recommended replacing the traditional 3-tier Panchayati Raj system with a 2-tier system consisting of Zila Parishad at the district level and Mandal Panchayat at the cluster level (covering a group of villages with 15,000 to 20,000 population).
check_circle
Statement 2 is CORRECT: It recommended that Nyaya Panchayats should be kept strictly as separate bodies from developmental panchayats and presided over by a qualified judge to maintain the dignity and impartiality of local judicial administration.
cancel
Statement 3 is INCORRECT: Unlike earlier committees (such as the Balwant Rai Mehta Committee which favored party-less democracy), the Ashok Mehta Committee was the very first committee to explicitly recommend official participation of political parties at all levels of Panchayat elections with party symbols and open contests. Statement 3 asserts that it *prohibited* political participation, which is factually opposite to the report's groundbreaking recommendation.
library_books Citation Source: Ashok Mehta Committee Report (1978), Ministry of Agriculture and Irrigation, Government of India, Chapter IV, Para 4.3.

Ashok Mehta (1977) = 2-Tier + Open Party Participation + Constitutional Status + Nyaya Panchayats.
